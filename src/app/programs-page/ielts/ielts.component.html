IELTS (International English Language Testing System)

The International English Language Testing System is an international standardized test of English language proficiency for non-native English language speakers. It is jointly managed by the British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia and Cambridge Assessment English,[6] and was established in 1989. IELTS is one of the major English Language tests in the world. There are two modules and an additional test.

What is the IELTS test for?

IELTS Academic - is designed to measure your English language proficiency and to assess if it is suitable for an academic environment. The test reflects aspects of academic language and evaluates whether you're ready to begin training or studying in English.

Who is eligible for IELTS test?

The only eligibility for the IELTS exam in India and across the globe is that a candidate needs to be at least 16 years. Anybody aged 16 or above can take this examination, irrespective of their gender, nationality, or background. Exceptions are only made when a school needs the IELTS scores for admission purposes.

Key points about the IELTS

Purpose: IELTS is designed to assess the English language proficiency of non-native English speakers who want to study, work, or migrate to English-speaking countries.

Types of IELTS: There are two main types of IELTS exams:

IELTS Academic: Designed for individuals seeking admission to higher education institutions and professional registration in English-speaking countries.
IELTS General Training: Primarily used for immigration, work, and training programs. It is also suitable for candidates planning to study at below-degree level.
Sections: Both IELTS types have four components, which evaluate different language skills:

Listening: Assessing the ability to understand spoken English in various contexts.
Reading: Evaluating reading comprehension skills using academic or everyday texts.
Writing: Testing the ability to express ideas in written form through essays, reports, or letters.
Speaking: Evaluating the ability to communicate verbally, express opinions, and engage in a conversation with an examiner.
Score Range: IELTS scores are reported on a 0-9 band scale for each component, and an overall band score is calculated as the average of the four component scores. Different institutions and organizations may have specific band score requirements.
